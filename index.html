Spicy Tokyo Miso Ramen

Rich umami pork bone broth infused with fermented spicy red miso, chashu pork, seasoned soft boiled ramen egg & nori.

⭐ 4.9
Prep & Cook ⏱️ 30 Mins
Yield 🍽️ 2 Servings
Level 🟡 Intermediate

🥗 Required Ingredients

  • 200g Fresh Japanese Ramen Noodles
  • 3 tbsp Red Miso Paste & Chili Oil (Rayu)
  • 2 Ajitsuke Tamago (Marinated Soft-Boiled Eggs)
  • 4 slices Slow-Braised Chashu Pork Belly
  • Finely Sliced Scallions & Roasted Nori Sheet

🔥 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Broth Base: Whisk miso paste, sesame oil, and garlic chili paste in a hot pot with piping hot dashi chicken broth.
  2. Boil Noodles: Drop fresh ramen noodles into rapidly boiling water for exactly 90 seconds until al dente.
  3. Assemble & Garnish: Pour hot broth into bowls, nestle noodles, top with chashu pork, halved jammy eggs, sliced scallions, and a sheet of nori.
